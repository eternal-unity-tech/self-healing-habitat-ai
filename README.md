# Self-Healing Habitat AI

**AI-2036 Off-Grid Sustaining Systems**  
Repository: [eternal-unity-tech/self-healing-habitat-ai](https://github.com/eternal-unity-tech/self-healing-habitat-ai)

## Vision
Create regenerative habitats that detect damage, grow or repair themselves using living mycelium systems, and operate fully off-grid under AI orchestration.

## Core Technologies
- Mycelium-based composites as primary structural and insulating material
- Living or semi-living mycelium layers capable of crack-filling and adaptive response
- Edge AI for structural health monitoring, growth control, and environmental regulation
- Modular, locally grown components from agricultural and construction waste
- Closed-loop integration with water, energy, food, and waste subsystems

## Current Focus Areas
1. **Material Systems** – Mycelium composites, hybrid skins, self-healing formulations
2. **Sensing & Digital Twins** – Continuous structural and environmental monitoring
3. **Growth & Repair AI** – Closed-loop bioreactor and in-situ repair control
4. **Habitat Architecture** – Modular domes, panels, and free-form grown structures optimized for off-grid resilience
